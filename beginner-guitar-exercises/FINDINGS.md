# Beginner Guitar Exercise Books — Free PDFs (verified)

All files downloaded from the author's / publisher's own free-download pages
and opened to verify contents. Prefer these over Scribd / aggregator copies.

## 1. Guitar Workout — Kenny Mann (Guitar Alliance) ⭐ best exercise-only book
- Local: `guitar-workout.pdf` (731 KB)
- Official free download: https://guitaralliance.com/dmdocuments/guitar%20workout.pdf
- 75 exercises: chromatic alternate picking (1-12), finger isolation (13-24),
  legato hammer-on/pull-off (25-47), pentatonic (48-60), endurance (61-68),
  strength (69-71), finger confusion (72-75). Metronome-based, TAB diagrams.
- Note: ©2013 Guitar Alliance, hosted free by the publisher.

## 2. Finger Exercises for Guitar — Mi Guitarra Eléctrica
- Local: `finger-exercises-for-guitar.pdf` (590 KB)
- Source: https://miguitarraelectrica.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/finger-exercises-for-guitar-pdf.pdf
- 4 alternate-picking exercises (1234, pinky, finger independence, high-E),
  5-10 min/day, TAB included. Short and perfect for week 1-2.

## 3. Beginner Guitar Method Book — Kale Good (Good Music Academy)
- Local: `beginner-guitar-method-book-kale-good.pdf` (1.25 MB)
- Official free copy: https://goodmusicacademy.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Beginner-Guitar-Method-Book.pdf
- Author created it for his students, posted free 2018. 41 pp.: adduction /
  finger-independence, tuning, metronome, chromatic permutations, first
  chords (A/D/E, G), strumming, alternate picking, first scale.
  More classical-leaning than the others.

## 4. Beginner Guitar Written Lesson Manual — David Taub (Next Level Guitar)
- Local: `beginner-guitar-manual-nextlevel.pdf` (698 KB)
- Source: https://nextlevelguitar.com/resources/pdf/manual_beginner_guitar.pdf
- 36 pp.: parts of guitar, first 3 chords (Em/Asus2/Am9), fret-hand technique,
  strum patterns (d/d/u/u/d/u etc.), 12 open chords, chord-change system
  (shared fingers / cluster / slide), fretboard notes. Copyright ©2011,
  hosted free by the author.

## 5. Ultimate Beginner Guide — Guitar Tricks (free lead-magnet)
- Local: `ultimate-beginner-guide-guitartricks.pdf` (3.7 MB)
- Source: https://www.guitartricks.com/images/ultimate-beginner-guide/GT-Ebook-UltimateBeginnerGuide.pdf
- 22 pp.: 10 beginner mistakes, how to practice, 60-min sample routine,
  beginner chord chart. Promotional (25% off code inside), but free.

## 6. Practice sheets (1-4 pp., print-and-go)
- `riff-beginner-practice-sheet.pdf` — 15-min session: spider 1-string,
  Em→Am change, down-up strumming. https://riff.quest/downloads/beginner-guitar-practice-sheet.pdf
- `pb-beginner-chords.pdf` — Pebber Brown beginner chord diagrams (4 pp.)
- `pb-spider-exercise.pdf`, `pb-left-hand-basics-101.pdf` — PB Guitar Studio
  free exercise worksheets. Index: https://www.pbguitarstudio.com/GuitarLessonPDF.html

## Also free, chapter-by-chapter (classical)
Jeffrey Goodman — *A Beginner's Guide to the Classical Guitar*, free chapters:
https://www.jeffreygoodmanmusic.com/beginning-guitar-free-pdfs-for-download
(e.g. Guitar Chords, Left Hand Technique, The Art of Practicing)

## Skipped on purpose
Scribd uploads ("Beginner Guitar Exercises", "Guitar for Beginners PDF") and
InfoBooks "21 free guitar books" — login-walled / copyright unclear, and
usually copies of commercial books. The ones above are author-hosted.

## Suggested order
1. Finger Exercises (4 exercises, daily) + Riff 15-min sheet
2. Next Level manual (chords + strumming + chord changes)
3. Guitar Workout (when 1234 is clean — start Ex. 1-5 at 60 bpm)
4. Kale Good method (finger independence / alternate picking depth)

# Batch 2 — found on "find more"

## 7. Classical Guitar Method Vol. 1 — Bradford Werner (102 pp.) ⭐ best structured beginner book
- Local: `classical-guitar-method-vol1-werner.pdf` (1.58 MB)
- Official free PDF: https://www.thisisclassicalguitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Classical-Guitar-Method-Vol1-Werner.pdf
- Page: https://www.thisisclassicalguitar.com/free-classical-guitar-method-book-pdf/
- CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, free to share with credit. Progressive method + chord/fingerstyle
  section + technique routines (right/left hand, chromatic scales) at the back.
  Free video lesson for nearly every page on the site.

## 8. Late-Beginner Collection — Bradford Werner (supplement to Vol. 1 & 2)
- Local: `late-beginner-collection-werner.pdf` (974 KB)
- Source: https://www.thisisclassicalguitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Late-Beginner-Collection-Free.pdf
- 20+ pieces: Cano, Mertz, Carcassi, Carulli, Pratten, Sagreras (48 & 61), etc.

## 9. Sagreras — First Lessons for Guitar, Book 1 (public domain, IMSLP)
- Local: `sagreras-first-lessons-book1-imslp.pdf` (833 KB)
- Source (IMSLP): https://s9.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/1/19/IMSLP610099-PMLP417963-Sagreras_First_Lessons_for_Guitar_Book1.pdf
- Julio Salvador Sagreras (1879-1942), Lessons 1-86 + scales. The classic
  absolute-beginner classical method: open strings → scales → chromatic →
  chords/arpeggios → ligados. Marieh 2020 Copyleft re-engraving on IMSLP.

## 10. Alternate Picking for Guitar Explained — C. E. Martin (jazzimproviser.com)
- Local: `alternate-picking-guitar-explained-free-ebook.pdf` (4.15 MB)
- Source: https://jazzimproviser.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/alternate-picking-guitar-explained-free-ebook.pdf
- Free ebook (©2018, author-hosted): pick grips, wrist mechanics, string-crossing
  "skip" concept, even/odd groupings, tetra-chords, metronome work. Technique
  depth, not a full beginner course.

## 11. Guitar Exercises Made Simple — Maxwell Powers (Free Guitar Source)
- Local: `guitar-exercises-made-simple.pdf` (9.8 MB)
- Source: https://guitaralliance.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Guitar-Exercises-Made-Simple-Play-Better-Guitar-Guaranteed.pdf
- ©2019, author-hosted free. 8 chapters × 15 exercises: warm-up, string skipping,
  bending, hammer-on/pull-off, slide, arpeggios, rhythm. TAB + metronome, 5-10 min/day.
  More rock/blues oriented than Guitar Workout.

## 12. Playing Guitar: A Beginner's Guide — Darrin Koltow (MaximumMusician)
- Local: `beginner-guide-michael-powers.pdf` (425 KB) [file hosted at michaelpowersmusic.com]
- Source: https://michaelpowersmusic.com/Guitarlessonmedia/BegGuideGtr.pdf
- 41 pp.: tuning, TAB/chord diagrams, first song (I-vi-IV-V), basic + barre chords,
  strumming, alternate picking, bass-note picking, pentatonic improvising,
  major scales, playing by ear, reading music. ©2002, author-hosted free.

## 13. Beginning Guitar for the Classroom — Lou Warde, 17th ed. (113 pp.)
- Local: `beginning-book-guitar-classroom.pdf` (7.5 MB)
- Source: https://www.theguitarclassroom.com/uploads/6/6/7/2/66720013/beginning_book.pdf
- ©2024 Platso Music, hosted free on author's classroom site. Full curriculum:
  popular chords/songs (Get Back → Stairway to Heaven), reading music duets,
  classical arpeggio patterns (p-i-m, p-i-m-a), guitar quartets, sight-reading.
  Note: companion eBook/audio at TheGuitarClassroom.com.
